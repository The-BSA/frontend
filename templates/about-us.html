About us and our vision

A place for every Bluesky cohort to meet, connect, and learn from each other.

Our mission

Connect people in one single place, where the distance between knowing someone new, is just clicks away. We want to help the internal networking of our program, so everyone has equal opportunities to meet eachother.

Why we built this

 A new cohort joins our program every year. When we arrived, most of us only knew the students in our own cohort. The groups before us felt like strangers, even though we share the same program, the same classes, and many of the same goals.

 Professional networking sites are built for everyone, which makes it hard to find the people who are right next to you. So we created a mini LinkedIn made only for our program's cohorts. Here you can share who you are, see what other students are working on, and reach out to someone you would not have met otherwise.

Who this site is for

New students

Find friendly faces early and learn from students who started before you.

Current cohorts

Share your skills and interests, and connect with students across every cohort.

Past students

Stay in touch with the program and offer advice to the students who follow you.

What we stand for

  • Connection. Every student should know someone outside their own cohort.
  • Support. Students help each other with advice, projects, and opportunities.
  • Community. Each new cohort adds to the network instead of starting over.
